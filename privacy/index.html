Privacy Policy — Pixel Stones

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Pixel Stones is a puzzle game. This policy explains what data the game and its partners handle. The game has no accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases.

Data the game itself keeps

Your progress (completed levels, the level in progress) and settings (sound) are saved only on your device or in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in the browser, deletes them.

Advertising on Android and iOS

The mobile versions show ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and approximate location, and may use cookies or similar technologies. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy" button in the main menu. You can also reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Web version

When you play on a web portal (such as Poki), the portal shows the ads and collects its own analytics under its own privacy policy. The game itself sends nothing to us.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; ads are requested with family-safe settings and adult, gambling and similar categories are blocked.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
